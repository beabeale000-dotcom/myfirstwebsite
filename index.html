Discover the tastiest eats, coastal fish tacos, and cozy cafes in San Luis Obispo and surrounding beach towns!

Avila Beach Pier Tacos 🐟

⭐ 4.9 / 5.0
📍 Avila Beach • 15 Mins from Downtown SLO

Nothing beats fresh grilled rockfish tacos eaten right on the sand. Grab a seat on the patio overlooking the bay, catch the ocean breeze, and enjoy house-made salsa with freshly pressed corn tortillas.

Local Tip: Head over right around 5 PM for golden hour photos and easy parking along Front Street!

Fresh Seafood Ocean View Outdoor Patio

Downtown SLO Espresso & Acai 🌺

⭐ 4.8 / 5.0
📍 Higuera Street • Downtown San Luis Obispo

Before hitting the coast, fuel up with a loaded acai bowl stacked with local berries, coconut flakes, and honey drizzle. Pair it with a cold brew lavender latte for the ultimate morning pick-me-up.

Local Tip: Visit on Thursday evenings to walk directly over to the famous SLO Farmers Market right outside the door!

Breakfast Acai Bowls Coffee

Pismo Chowder & Clam Shacks 🥣

⭐ 4.9 / 5.0
📍 Pismo Beach • 12 Mins South of SLO

Warm up after a morning surf session with a sourdough bread bowl brimming with rich, creamy clam chowder. A Central Coast classic that has been serving hungry beachgoers for decades.

Local Tip: Take your clam chowder bowl to-go and walk down the Pismo Pier to watch the surfers!
