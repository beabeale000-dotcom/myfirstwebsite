How to replace this page

  1. Copy what Gemini gives you with the copy button on the code block. Do not click and drag.
  2. Open index.html here and click the pencil icon.
  3. Ctrl+A (Cmd+A on Mac), then Delete. All of it. Yes, really.
  4. Paste, commit, refresh.
